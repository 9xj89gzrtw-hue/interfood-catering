## 5. LEGAL REQUIREMENTS FOR CATERING WEBSITES IN RUSSIA

### 5.1 Mandatory Information (149-ФЗ "Об информации...")

**For ООО (LLC)**:
- Полное фирменное наименование + организационно-правовая форма
- ОГРН (Основной государственный регистрационный номер)
- ИНН (Идентификационный номер налогоплательщика)
- КПП (Код причины постановки на учет)
- Юридический адрес
- Email для связи
- Сведения о представителе

**For ИП (Individual Entrepreneur)**:
- ФИО полностью
- Статус «ИП»
- ОГРНИП (Основной государственный регистрационный номер ИП)
- ИНН
- Адрес регистрации (место жительства)
- Данные лицензии (если деятельность лицензируется)

**For Самозанятый (Self-employed/НПД)**:
- ФИО
- Статус «плательщик налога на профессиональный доход»
- ИНН
- Контактные данные

### 5.2 Personal Data Law (152-ФЗ "О персональных данных")

**Required documents on site:**
1. **Политика обработки персональных данных (Политика конфиденциальности)**
   - Must be publicly accessible
   - Must include: purposes of data processing, specific data types, legal basis, methods and timeframes, protection measures, third-party transfers, subject's rights
   - Link must be in site footer on every page

2. **Согласие на обработку персональных данных**
   - Checkbox in every form that collects personal data
   - Must NOT be pre-checked (Роскомнадзор: active user action required)
   - Must include clickable link to Privacy Policy next to the checkbox
   - Must log: IP address, timestamp, source, full text of consent

3. **New requirements since Sept 2022 / March 2023:**
   - Data of Russian citizens must be processed on servers physically located in Russia
   - Cross-border transfer requires separate consent
   - Mandatory notification of Roskomnadzor before starting data processing

4. **Updated fines since May 30, 2025:**
   - Fines up to 15,000,000 ₽ for non-compliance
   - Old forms and improper consent = violation
   - Must fix consent collection on all forms

### 5.3 Cookie Consent
- Not as strict as GDPR, but Роскомнадзор recommends obtaining consent
- A cookie banner/notification is recommended (not strictly required by law yet)
- Best practice: show a notice bar stating "We use cookies" with link to Privacy Policy
- Must not pre-accept cookies for the user

### 5.4 User Agreement / Terms (Пользовательское соглашение)
- **For e-commerce (ordering services online)**: Public offer (публичная оферта) required
  - Must include: description of service, price, delivery/payment terms, return policy
  - Acceptance by user action (clicking "Order" button)
- **For informational/corporate sites**: Agreement on site use is sufficient but recommended
- Must comply with Закон «О защите прав потребителей» (Consumer Protection Law)

### 5.5 Other Mandatory Elements
- **Реквизиты** in footer or on dedicated page
- **Правила возврата** (Return policy) — for online sales
- **Способы оплаты** (Payment methods) disclosure
- **Маркировка рекламы** — all advertising must be clearly labeled (38-ФЗ «О рекламе»)

### 5.6 Placement Best Practices
- All legal documents linked from **footer of every page**
- Key documents (Privacy Policy, Terms) linked **next to form submission buttons**
- All documents must open in separate, easy-to-read pages
- Recommended audit cycle: quarterly review of all legal pages

### 5.7 Quick Legal Checklist for SPb Catering Website

```
☐ Footer: ИНН, ОГРН/ОГРНИП, legal name, address
☐ Footer: Links to Privacy Policy, User Agreement, Terms
☐ Separate page: Политика конфиденциальности (152-ФЗ compliant)
☐ Separate page: Пользовательское соглашение / Оферта
☐ Every form: Unchecked checkbox + link to Privacy Policy
☐ Every form: Consent text clearly states what data is collected
☐ Cookie notification banner (recommended)
☐ Return/refund policy page (if taking online payments)
☐ Roskomnadzor notification filed (if processing personal data)
☐ All data stored on Russian servers
☐ Advertising clearly labeled (if applicable)
```
