A Story of Love & Food

GET IN TOUCH

Exceptional Catering & Hospitality in New York City

We don’t just cook food — we grow it, compose it, and bring it to life like a performance.

For more than 40 years, Great Performances has been a leading catering and hospitality company in New York City, producing corporate events, cultural galas, weddings, and private celebrations across the city. Our chefs create seasonal menus while our planning and service teams deliver events with disciplined execution and genuine hospitality.

Our Food

Great food begins with great ingredients.

Our chefs draw from the seasons, New York’s global foodways, and the harvests from our own 60-acre organic Katchkie Farm in the Hudson Valley. From May through October, up to 30% of the produce used in our kitchens comes directly from our farm, grounding our menus in the rhythm of the season.

Stunning Venues

Host your event in one of New York’s most remarkable spaces.

As the exclusive or preferred caterer for many of the city’s leading cultural institutions and event venues, we bring deep familiarity with spaces that define New York’s event landscape.

From The Plaza Hotel and Jazz at Lincoln Center to gardens, museums, and historic landmarks, each setting provides a stage for unforgettable gatherings.

Our Story

Life Happens Around Food®and that’s where our story begins.

Founded in 1980 to support women working in the arts, Great Performances grew from a small staffing company into one of New York’s leading hospitality organizations grounded in food, community, and culture.
